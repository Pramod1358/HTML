Data Science

Data Science is a field that combines , mathematics, programming, and domain knowledge to extract useful insights from large amounts of data. It helps organizations understand patterns, make predictions, and support better decision-making. Data scientists collect data from various sources, clean and organize it, and analyze it using tools such as Python, SQL, Excel, and R. Machine learning is an important part of data science, allowing computers to learn from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed for every task.
visualization tools such as Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Power BI help present complex information through charts and graphs. Data Science is widely used in healthcare, finance, marketing, education, e-commerce, and many other industries. With the rapid growth of digital data, data science[1] has become an important technology for solving real-world problems and improving business performance.
